WHY PRATIRUP

Move from reactive monitoring to predictive intelligence.

Threshold-based monitoring tells engineers when a value has already crossed a limit. PRATIRUP is designed to understand trends, compare expected and actual behaviour, identify degradation and support maintenance decisions before a serious fault develops.

CONVENTIONAL

Reactive Engine Monitoring

  • 01Fixed threshold alerts
  • 02Focus on current values
  • 03Limited degradation context
  • 04Manual interpretation
  • 05No direct RUL estimate

PLATFORM CAPABILITIES

Built for continuous engine-health awareness.

Every layer of PRATIRUP is focused on making telemetry more useful to the engineer.

01

Real-Time Telemetry

Process engine parameters continuously from simulated sources today and real hardware interfaces later.

02

Anomaly Detection

Identify unusual behaviour before fixed safety thresholds are necessarily crossed.

03

Degradation Tracking

Monitor slow changes in thermal, vibration and performance behaviour across operating history.

04

RUL Prediction

Estimate remaining useful life and support maintenance planning using health trends.

05

Mission Replay

Replay recorded missions and inspect engine behaviour through changing mission phases.

06

Predictive Maintenance

Translate engine-health intelligence into a practical inspection or maintenance recommendation.
